# Tiwana ERP overview

Tiwana ERP is a construction ERP covering Projects, Drawings, BOQ, Procurement, Inventory, Finance, Site Execution, Labour, Equipment, Quality, Planning, HR and Reports.

## Getting started
1. Sign in with your company account.
2. Open a project you can access (project visibility is enforced on every screen and assistant query).
3. Use module menus for Drawings → BOQ → Procurement → Inventory → Finance as your workflow requires.
4. Approvals never bypass the approval engine — the Smart Assistant is **read-only** and cannot create or approve documents for you.

## Smart Assistant
Ask how-to questions (for example “How do I create a BOQ?”) or live summaries (for example “Total PO amount this month”).
Unsupported or unclear questions return suggestions instead of guesses.

## Imports
Many masters and transactional drafts support Excel/CSV import with Download Template.
**Drawing Management does not support spreadsheet import** — upload drawing files instead.

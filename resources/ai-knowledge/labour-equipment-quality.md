# Labour, Equipment & Quality

## Labour
- Record attendance and labour payments under the project Labour module.
- Advances and payments follow the existing approval / mark-paid workflow.
- The assistant can explain navigation; live labour cost analytics are limited to supported summaries.

## Equipment
- Register equipment, log usage and repairs from the Equipment module.
- Cross-project moves follow inventory / asset rules already configured in the ERP.

## Quality
- Raise inspections and NCRs from Quality.
- Drawing approvals remain under Drawing Management (separate from quality inspections).

## Site Diary / DPR
- Site Diary and Daily Progress Reports are entered per project for authorized site users.

# Finance

## Expenses
1. Project → Expenses → Create.
2. Enter date, lines and amounts → Save draft → Submit → Approve.
3. Approved expenses post cost to the project per existing rules.

## Vendor bills & payments
1. Enter vendor bills (invoices) against PO/GRN or as direct bills.
2. Submit for approval. After approval, record payments against the bill.
3. Outstanding = net payable minus paid amount on approved / partially paid bills.
4. View vendor payments and unpaid bills from Finance (requires vendor bill permissions).

## Credit / debit notes
Use the finance document flows already configured — the Smart Assistant cannot post them.

## Reports
Monthly purchase, expense and project expenditure summaries are available when you have financial view permissions.

The Smart Assistant is read-only: it cannot approve payments or post invoices.
